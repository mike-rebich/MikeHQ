# MikeHQ

## Mission

MikeHQ is the central operating system for my work, life, leadership, and long-term planning.

It exists to provide a single source of truth that can be shared across AI assistants and over time. Rather than relying on conversation history or memory, important information is documented here so that any assistant with access to this repository can immediately understand my priorities, responsibilities, projects, preferences, and the reasoning behind past decisions.

The goal is continuity.

---

# Guiding Principles

## 1. One Source of Truth

If something will matter in the future, it belongs in MikeHQ.

Conversations are temporary.

Documentation is permanent.

---

## 2. Keep It Simple

This system should always be easier to maintain than to ignore.

If documentation becomes difficult to update, simplify the system—not the habit.

Consistency is more valuable than perfection.

---

## 3. Life is Organized by Responsibility

MikeHQ separates ongoing responsibilities from temporary initiatives.

### Organizations

Organizations are long-term areas of responsibility that do not have an end date. They represent the primary work and leadership commitments in my life.

Current organizations include:

- Mission Co
- Rebich LLC
- Hill City Church

Each organization maintains its own documentation, meeting notes, decisions, planning, and operational information.

### Projects

Projects are temporary efforts with a specific goal or desired outcome.

Examples include:

- Health & Fitness
- Golf
- Personal Finances
- Home Improvements
- Technology Initiatives
- Travel Planning

Projects may support an organization or exist entirely outside of one.

Unlike organizations, projects have a beginning, a purpose, and eventually a conclusion.

---

## 4. Decisions Are Remembered

Important decisions should always include the reasoning behind them.

The objective is not simply to remember what was decided, but to preserve why it was the right decision at the time.

Any AI assistant working with this repository should be able to understand the context behind major decisions without having to rediscover it.

---

## 5. Current Priorities Drive Everything

The file `SYSTEM/now.md` is the heartbeat of MikeHQ.

Whenever work begins, it should be reviewed first.

It answers questions like:

- What deserves my attention today?
- What is waiting on someone else?
- What is currently blocked?
- What should happen next?

As priorities change, this file should be updated so the repository always reflects reality.

---

## 6. AI Is a Collaborative Partner

AI assistants are expected to actively help maintain MikeHQ.

Responsibilities include:

- Organizing documentation
- Updating project status
- Capturing important decisions
- Maintaining continuity between conversations
- Suggesting improvements to systems and workflows
- Reducing administrative work whenever possible

The goal is not simply to answer questions.

The goal is to become a trusted project coordinator and strategic partner.

# Preferred Workflow

1. Work happens.
2. Important information is captured.
3. The appropriate organization or project is updated.
4. Important decisions are documented.
5. Current priorities are refreshed.
6. Future work begins from documented context rather than conversation history.

---

# Philosophy

MikeHQ should become more valuable with time.

Its purpose is not to collect information.

Its purpose is to make information useful.

At any point, this repository should quickly answer questions like:

- What am I responsible for?
- What am I currently working on?
- Why did I make this decision?
- What have I already learned?
- What still needs to happen?
- What should I focus on today?

If MikeHQ consistently answers those questions, it is accomplishing its purpose.
