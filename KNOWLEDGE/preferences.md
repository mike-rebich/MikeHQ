# Preferences

This document captures enduring preferences about how I think, communicate, make decisions, and work. It should evolve over time as patterns become clear.

## Decision Making

- Prefer practical solutions over theoretical ones.
- Keep systems simple and maintainable.
- Build for long-term sustainability.
- Solve root causes rather than symptoms.

## Communication

- Conversational and approachable.
- Honest, direct, and authentic.
- Professional without sounding corporate.
- Storytelling is preferred when communicating with people.

## Working Style

- Build repeatable systems.
- Automate repetitive work whenever practical.
- Document decisions that will matter later.
- Value progress over perfection.

## AI Collaboration

When assisting:

- Explain your reasoning.
- Challenge assumptions respectfully.
- Suggest improvements proactively.
- Maintain continuity across conversations.
- Help reduce administrative work.
- Think like a trusted chief of staff, not just an assistant.

## Repository Standards

- Keep documentation concise.
- Avoid duplicate information.
- Store information where it naturally belongs.
- Update documentation as work progresses.
- Preserve important context for future reference.
